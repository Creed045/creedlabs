# CreedLabs — Design Context (`impeccable-design` + `taste-frontend`)

## 1. Product, Audience & Core Job
- **Product:** CreedLabs Restaurant Ordering, Kitchen Display System (`/kitchen`), and Back Office (`/admin`).
- **Audience:** Dine-in & delivery guests ordering Nigerian classics, smoky proteins, and global favourites in Lagos/Nigeria, plus line cooks and restaurant managers.
- **One Job:** Let guests browse dishes, customize heat/sides, and pay (single or multi-account Squad split bill) in under 45 seconds while giving kitchen and admin staff zero-clutter real-time operational control.

## 2. Art Direction & Dials (`taste-frontend`)
- **Art Direction:** **"Obsidian & Cobalt Culinary Editorial"** — Deep slate/obsidian surfaces (`#0a0d14` / `#07090e`) paired with crisp warm-tinted daylight paper (`#f5f7fb`), editorial serif mastheads (`Instrument Serif`), tabular numeric utility (`DM Sans` + `JetBrains Mono`), and 60fps Apple Liquid Glass tactile controls.
- **Dials:** `VARIANCE: 7` (asymmetric editorial hero + featured signature spotlight card breaking the uniform 3-card grid) · `MOTION: 6` (Premium/Calm `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` entrance choreography, 140ms exits, GPU-only `transform`/`opacity`) · `DENSITY: 4` on Customer Menu, `DENSITY: 8` on Kitchen KDS & Admin POS.

## 3. Palette & Typography Tokens
- **Primary Accent:** Cobalt Blue `#2563eb` (Light) / `#3b82f6` (Dark)
- **Obsidian Ink:** `#0a0d14` (Light ink / Dark surface base `#07090e`)
- **Semantic Status:** Emerald `#15803d` (`--ok`), Amber `#b45309` (`--warn`), Crimson `#dc2626` (`--bad`)
- **Typography:**
  - Display: `'Instrument Serif', Georgia, serif` (`clamp(2.5rem, 6vw, 3.75rem)`, tracking `-0.02em`)
  - UI & Body: `'DM Sans', sans-serif` with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`
  - Ticket IDs, Timers & Monetary Breakdown: `'JetBrains Mono', monospace`

## 4. Anti-Slop Guardrails
- No emoji icons anywhere in UI chrome (1.75px stroke SVGs only).
- Never render a uniform 3×N grid as the entire page without visual hierarchy: highlight the signature dish in `Popular` view and expose a quick-jump Chef's Signature spotlight in the masthead.
- Every interactive control meets `>= 44px` touch target or comfortable hit area, has visible `:focus-visible`, and respects `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
